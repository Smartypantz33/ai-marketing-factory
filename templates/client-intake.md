# Client intake questionnaire

Send this before the first meeting, or work through it together in the discovery call. Save the completed copy as `outputs/<client-slug>/YYYY-MM-DD-client-intake.md`.

## The business
1. Business name, website and social accounts:
2. What do you sell, in one sentence?
3. Where do you serve customers (area, online, both)?
4. How long have you been in business, and why did you start?
5. What makes you different from the alternatives your customers consider?

## Customers
6. Who usually *contacts* you first? Who *decides* and who *pays*?
7. What's going on in their life when they reach out?
8. What questions or worries do they raise most often?
9. Where do most of your customers find you today?

## Goals
10. What would make the next 3 months a success? (Be as specific as you can.)
11. What have you tried in marketing before? What worked, what didn't?
12. Is there a season, date or event we should plan around?

## Practicalities
13. Monthly budget for tools and ads (a range is fine):
14. Who approves content, and how fast can they turn it around?
15. Brand assets available: logo, colours, fonts, photos, videos, testimonials (with permission)?
16. Anything we must never say or do?

## Permissions
17. Can we use customer reviews or stories? Which ones, with what permission?
18. Are you comfortable with AI-assisted content? Any disclosure preferences?
