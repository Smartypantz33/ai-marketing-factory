## 1. Brand story

- Founder **Lisa Fisher** came to senior move management through volunteering with Better at Home. A friendship there showed her how many seniors face major transitions without practical or emotional support. (W)
- Named in honour of that friend, **Raija**, whose name means **"guiding light."** Rya lights the way and walks alongside; it doesn't take over. (W)
- Lisa: 20+ years in education and student support; organizing, logistics and care-based communication; experience with elders; board member of Making Connections Community Society and Squamish Seniors Living Society. (W)
- Origin image: Lisa's photo of herself with Raija in the Sea to Sky mountains. Use it on About pages and in founder-story content. (L)
- Naming: "Rya Rightsizing" in full on first reference; "Rya" after. Never "RYA," "Rya's Rightsizing," "Rya Right Sizing." Pronunciation to be confirmed with Lisa.

## 2. Purpose, promise, positioning

- **Primary tagline:** Compassionate rightsizing, downsizing + move services for seniors. (Adapted from Lisa's business card, which reads "Compassionate downsizing, decluttering + move services for seniors," to lead with rightsizing.)
- **Purpose:** Help seniors and their families move through change with dignity, care and confidence, so no one has to face it alone.
- **Promise:** We take care of every detail, practical and emotional, so your family can have peace of mind.

**Services (W):**

| Service | Length | Investment |
|---|---|---|
| Complimentary consultation, in-home or virtual (includes the complimentary guide *Making Space for What Matters*) | 45 min | Complimentary |
| Needs assessment | 60 min | $80 |
| Senior rightsizing and downsizing | Three-hour blocks of time or more | From $240 |
| Senior moving | Three-hour blocks of time or more | From $240 |

Rates from $80/hr per specialist. Support can be short-term or longer-term. Every service includes a dedicated family liaison (single point of contact).

- **Copy rules:** say "complimentary consultation, in-home or virtual" (never "free"); say "three-hour blocks of time or more" (never "sessions" or "minimum"); never say "no long-term contract" or "all-or-nothing."
- **Positioning (R):** For families in North & West Vancouver and the Sea to Sky corridor helping an aging parent rightsize, downsize or move, Rya Rightsizing is the compassionate, local move specialist that handles both the logistics and the emotions, so every family member feels supported, informed and at peace, even from a distance. Unlike a moving or junk-removal company, Rya treats a lifetime of belongings as a story to honour, not a load to clear.

## 3. Who we serve

- **Adult child (often the buyer):** busy, maybe far away; feels guilty, stretched, worried; wants a trustworthy local person and clear communication.
- **Senior (person at the centre):** staying in place, moving smaller, or to senior living; overwhelmed, attached, possibly grieving or limited in mobility; wants respect, their own pace, to keep what matters.
- **Secondary:** estate executors and grieving families; individuals decluttering (incl. storage units, hoarding situations); referral partners (realtors, senior living, movers, non-profits).
- **Area:** North & West Vancouver and the Sea to Sky corridor (Squamish, Whistler and between); office in Squamish. North & West Vancouver is a key target; use #NorthAndWestVancouver on social. Don't say "Metro Vancouver."

## 4. Brand beliefs
1. A move is an emotional journey, not just logistics.
2. Rightsizing, not just downsizing: the right balance for this chapter, not simply less.
3. Dignity is non-negotiable.
4. No one should face big transitions alone.
5. Meaningful order creates calm.
6. Your pace, your plan. No pressure; three-hour blocks of time or more, short-term or longer-term.
7. Belongings deserve a good next home (local donations, landfill diversion).
8. Families deserve peace of mind, even from afar.

## 5. Personality
Calm, capable friend who shows up with a plan, a kind word and packing tape. Balance of heart and competence.

- Warm, never syrupy · Capable, never clinical or bossy · Respectful, never condescending · Grounded, never alarmist · Hopeful, never falsely cheerful · Community-minded, never corporate.
- Archetype (R): Caregiver with a streak of Sage.

## 6. Voice (constant)
Compassionate · Reassuring · Respectful · Clear and practical · Hopeful.

- "We" for Rya, "you" for the reader. Short-to-medium sentences. Grade 7–8 reading level. Canadian spelling. Exclamation marks rarely. No emoji in service copy; sparing on social.

## 8. Language guide

- **Rightsizing first, downsizing second.** "Rightsizing" is the primary word for what Rya does. Use "downsizing" as a secondary term because it's what people search for: in page titles, headings, meta descriptions, URLs, hashtags and alongside rightsizing ("rightsizing and downsizing"). Where there's room, explain the difference: "We call it rightsizing, because it's not about having less."
- **Use:** complimentary consultation, in-home or virtual, blocks of time, rightsizing, downsizing (secondary), next chapter, at your pace, meaningful, dignity, peace of mind, honour, support, calm, manageable, thoughtful, compassionate, guide, liaison, heirlooms, belongings, older adults, seniors, your parent, investment.
- **Avoid:** junk (about belongings; "junk removal" as a service name is fine), purge, get rid of, elderly, "old folks," hoarder as a label, "stuff" in formal copy, cheap, fast/quick, cluttered mess, "before it's too late," free consultation, 3-hr minimum, sessions, no long-term contract, all-or-nothing, Metro Vancouver.
- **"+":** Lisa uses "+" in taglines and short headers. Fine there; use "and" in body copy.
- **Signature phrases (on site):** Guided by heart. Grounded in compassion. · Making space for what matters. · From overwhelming clutter to manageable, meaningful order. · We take care of all the details so that you can have peace of mind. · You don't have to navigate this alone. · The right balance between your space, your belongings, and the life you're living right now.
- **CTAs:** Book your complimentary consultation (primary; add "in-home or virtual" where space allows) · Let's talk about your family's move · Learn how we can help your family · Get the complimentary rightsizing guide. Avoid urgency CTAs.

## 12. Typography (L)

- **Headings and wordmark:** Adonis (Adobe Fonts), regular, sentence case.
- **Bold display:** Playfair Display (Google Fonts). Lisa uses it when she needs bold, because Adonis has no bold weight.
- **Body, nav, buttons:** Pontano Sans (Google Fonts). Buttons uppercase, ~0.1em letter-spacing (W).
- **No-cost substitute for Adonis (R):** Cormorant Garamond (Google Fonts, in Canva).
- **Rules:** body min 16 px screen / 11 pt print; sentence-case headlines; no long passages in serif or italics.
